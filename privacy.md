# Ruzaneh — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 9 November 2026*

Ruzaneh is a journal and bullet-journal app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by Kian Vosough. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **your journal stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not run a server, we do not collect analytics, and we never see your journal.**

## What Ruzaneh stores, and where

- **Your journal** (entries, log items, collections, goals, tags, stickers, templates, settings for your journals) is stored on your device and synced between your devices through **your own private iCloud database** (Apple CloudKit). Only you can read it. The developer has no access to it.
- **Photos and videos** you add to an entry are *linked* to your Photos library, not copied. Ruzaneh stores a small thumbnail with the entry. Pictures you drag in from files, and Day One imports, keep a reduced copy in your journal.
- **Apple Health** (iPhone and iPad only, and only if you turn it on): Ruzaneh *reads* steps, exercise minutes, active energy, distance, stand hours, sleep, mindful minutes, resting heart rate and workouts. It never writes to Apple Health. The numbers are kept **on that device only — they are never stored in iCloud** and never shared with anyone. If you tap "Add to log", Ruzaneh writes a note with that day's numbers into your journal; like any other note you write, that note is synced through your iCloud.
- **Location and weather** (optional, can be turned off in Settings → Journal): when you create an entry, Ruzaneh can note where you are and the weather. Your location is turned into a place name by Apple's location services, and weather comes from Apple's WeatherKit, which receives the location to return the forecast. The result is saved in your entry.
- **Calendars and reminders**: Ruzaneh reads and writes events through your device's own calendar accounts (Apple, Google, Exchange and others you have added to the system). Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device.
- **Passcode**: if you set an app passcode, it is stored as a salted hash in your iCloud Keychain so it works on all your devices.

## Signing in

You don't need an account to use Ruzaneh. If you choose to add one in Settings → Account (Sign in with Apple, Google, or a name and email), Ruzaneh saves your name and email in your journal, in your own iCloud. Google sign-in talks directly to Google; Ruzaneh reads only your name and email from it and keeps no Google token. No account is created on any server of ours — we don't have one. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account.

## Purchases

Ruzaneh Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Ruzaneh only checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your Apple ID.

## What we do not do

- No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
- No tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no advertising.
- No server: nothing you write is sent to the developer.

## Your control over your data

- **Export**: Settings → Data → Export journal saves everything as Markdown and JSON files you own.
- **Delete**: Settings → Reset → Delete everything removes your journal from your devices and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is stored on that device. Apple Health access can be removed any time in the Health app or Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
- **Permissions** (Photos, Location, Calendars, Notifications, Health) are asked for only when first needed and can be changed in your device's Settings.

## Children

Ruzaneh is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
